GaN SEGMENTED GATE DRIVER — FPGA SYNTHESIS
Two commands. Should take under ten minutes.

WHAT THIS IS
  A gate-driver controller in Verilog. It already simulates and passes its
  testbench; what we cannot do is synthesise it, because Vivado has no macOS
  build. We need Xilinx utilisation and timing numbers, nothing else.

STEP 1 — sanity check the RTL (optional, ~5 seconds, no Vivado needed)
  If you have Icarus Verilog:

    iverilog -g2012 -o tb seg_gate_ctrl_top_tb.v seg_gate_ctrl_top.v \
        seg_gate_ctrl.v dead_time_gen.v thermo_decode.v
    vvp tb

  Expected last line:  ALL CHECKS PASSED

STEP 2 — synthesis (this is the one we need)

    vivado -mode batch -source synth_only.tcl

  To target a specific part instead of the default xc7a35tcpg236-1:

    vivado -mode batch -source synth_only.tcl -tclargs xc7a100tcsg324-1

  Any Artix-7 part is fine. We are not targeting a board.

WHAT WE NEED BACK
  1. The console block printed at the end, headed "NUMBERS WE NEED".
     It has the LUT count, register count, and worst negative slack.
  2. The two files written to  vivado/build/ :
         utilization_synth.rpt
         timing_synth.rpt

  That is everything. If it errors, the error text is just as useful.

ONE THING THAT MATTERS
  The clock is constrained to 200 MHz (5 ns). That is a requirement, not a
  preference: the design counts clock cycles to make dead-time, and the
  shortest dead-time we use is 5 ns, which a 100 MHz clock cannot express.
  If timing fails at 200 MHz we need to know — that is a real result, not a
  problem with your setup.

WHY NOT build.tcl
  build.tcl is also in here. It runs place, route and bitstream generation,
  which need real package pins for a specific board. The pin assignments in
  the XDC are placeholders. Do not use build.tcl unless you want to target
  your own board — synth_only.tcl gives us what we need without any of that.

FILES
  seg_gate_ctrl.v        top-level controller
  dead_time_gen.v        complementary outputs, programmable dead time
  thermo_decode.v        slice count -> thermometer enables
  seg_gate_ctrl_top.v    synthesis wrapper
  seg_gate_ctrl_top_tb.v self-checking testbench for the wrapper
  seg_gate_ctrl.xdc      constraints (pin locations are placeholders)
  synth_only.tcl         <- run this
  build.tcl              full flow, needs your board's pins
