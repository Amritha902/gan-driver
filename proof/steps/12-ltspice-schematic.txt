STEP 12 -- the same circuit, drawn, in LTspice

This one is not a script: it is a schematic you open and press Run on.

    ~/gan-driver/ltspice/A_design_no_clamp_FAILS.asc        no clamp, 0 V
    ~/gan-driver/ltspice/B_design_clamp_on.asc              clamp on, 0 V
    ~/gan-driver/ltspice/C_design_clamp_and_neg_bias.asc    clamp on, -2 V

  1. Double-click any of the three. They open in LTspice.
  2. Simulate > Run.   (NOT Cmd+R -- in LTspice that rotates a component.)
  3. The waveform window shows V(hsg,sw), the gate of the device that is
     supposed to be OFF, over V(sw), the switch node that kicks it.
  4. View > SPICE Error Log prints the measurement.

WHAT COMES OUT -- LTspice 26.0.2, run 8 Sep 2026, against ngspice 47

    case                        LTspice        ngspice      difference
    A  no clamp, 0 V          +1.647556 V     +1.6486 V       1.0 mV
    B  clamp on, 0 V          +0.828274 V     +0.8302 V       1.9 mV
    C  clamp on, -2 V         -1.176857 V     -1.1757 V       1.2 mV

Two different simulators, the same schematic, agreeing to about a
millivolt. The threshold is 1.4 V, so A turns the device on and B and C
do not.

The devices are egan.lib's EGAN and the gate drivers are segdrv.lib's
SEGDRV -- the same subcircuits sim/dpt.cir uses. The drawing is not a
picture of the circuit, it IS the circuit.
