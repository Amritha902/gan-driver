  configuration                              mean cost

  All percentages below share ONE baseline: the median safe fixed
  word, cost 8.956. Mixing baselines is how a share stops meaning
  anything, so it is done once here and not again.

                                                     cost   of base
  (A) choosing a better FIXED word                  2.244     25.1%
  (B) ADAPTING it per operating point               0.347      3.9%
  total available                                   2.591     28.9%

  -> adaptation is 13.4 % of the total gain.
     The other 86.6 % needs no sensing, no lookup table, no controller.

  TWO words with a PERFECT selector (upper bound, not hardware):
    mean cost 6.462  -> closes 72 % of the adaptive gap
    split: {200V_2A_125C} vs the rest

  TWO words chosen by ONE REAL COMPARATOR (VBUS at 75):
    mean cost 6.553  -> closes 46 % of the adaptive gap
    split: {50V_2A_25C} vs {100V_10A_25C, 200V_10A_125C, 200V_2A_125C}

  The two differ, and the difference matters. The split above needs
  a selector no single threshold can build: the isolated corner shares
  its bus voltage, its load and its temperature with other corners, so
  picking it out takes TWO comparators. With one, 46 % of the adaptive
  gap closes, not 72 %.

  So the residual a full sense + ADC + lookup table must justify is
    7.3 % over a fixed word + ONE comparator   (13.4 % x 54.2 %)
    3.7 % over a fixed word + TWO comparators  (13.4 % x 27.8 %)
