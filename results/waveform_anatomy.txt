
  WAVEFORM ANATOMY -- sim/dpt.cir, the commutation edge

  THE AGGRESSOR   switch node V(sw)
    starts at                        106.0 V
    falls 90% -> 10% in               2.90 ns
    mean slew over that fall            29 V/ns
    peak slew (0.2 ns window)          124 V/ns
    -> this dv/dt drives i = C_GD * dv/dt into the OFF device's gate

  THE VICTIM      OFF-device gate V(gs)
                            rests at  peaks at      LIFT
    no clamp, 0 V rail       +0.005 V   +1.649 V   +1.643 V
    clamp on, -2 V rail      -1.941 V   -1.176 V   +0.765 V

    offset from the rail            -1.946 V
    lift removed by the clamp       -0.879 V  (1.643 -> 0.765)
    -> the rail lowers where it starts; the clamp shortens the lift.
       Two mechanisms, not one, and they are separately measurable.
