Capacitances vs V_DS  (pF)
        V_DS     C_iss     C_oss     C_rss
          0 V     500.0     950.0    150.00
         10 V     381.6     272.8     31.56
         50 V     361.6     123.7     11.65
        100 V     357.5      87.1      7.47
        150 V     355.8      70.9      5.75
  C_rss IS the crosstalk mechanism. A datasheet states it as a curve;
  this is that curve, and it is the first thing to check.

Gate charge to the 5 V rail: 7.3 nC
  sanity: C_iss(0 V) x 5 V = 2.5 nC, so a Q_g a few times that 
  is expected (the Miller plateau is the rest). consistent.

Third-quadrant drop at 10 A (GaN has no body diode)
    V_off = +0 V   V_SD = 2.74 V
    V_off = -2 V   V_SD = 4.74 V
  the -2 V rail costs +2.00 V of extra drop, and the physics says
  it should cost exactly 2.00 V. Agrees.
  This is the cost of the -2 V rail, and it is why our turn-on
  energy is higher than the base paper's at three of four corners.

HOW TO CHECK THIS, IN FIVE MINUTES
  Open the EPC2010C datasheet next to this table. R_DS(on) at 5 V,
  the C_rss curve at 100 V, and Q_g are the three that matter. If
  any of them is out by more than about 30 %, scripts/robust.py
  says which conclusions move -- and the +-50 % C_GD row there is
  the one to read first.
